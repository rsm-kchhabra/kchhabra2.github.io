    • Comprehensive Dashboard Write-Up: Prime Video Content Analysis This Power BI dashboard provides a multi-dimensional analysis of the Prime Video streaming catalog, showcasing critical insights into the platform’s content distribution, genre diversity, audience targeting, and global presence. The visual layout is clean and intuitive, enabling both executive and operational teams to quickly interpret the data. Key Highlights: •Content Volume: The platform hosts a total of 9,655 titles, encompassing 519 unique genres and 5,771 directors — signaling a rich and varied library. •Format Split: A significant 80.82% of the catalog consists of movies, with TV shows accounting for 19.18%, as depicted in the donut chart. This breakdown informs platform strategy in balancing long-form series versus feature-length content. •Temporal Distribution: The "Total Shows by Release Year" line graph highlights a dramatic growth in content acquisition and production since the early 2000s, peaking around 2020–2021. This trend is key for understanding Prime Video’s expansion curve and content freshness. •Age Ratings: The “Ratings by Total Shows” bar chart reveals that 13+, 16+, and All Ages are the most common ratings. This suggests a focus on teen and general-audience content, which has implications for family-friendly marketing and age-based recommendations. •Genre Insights: Drama, Comedy, and genre hybrids like “Drama, Suspense” are the top-performing categories. These insights can help content acquisition and original production teams prioritize investment in high-demand genres. •Geographic Distribution: The map visual for “Total Shows by Country” reflects strong content sourcing from North America, Europe, and parts of Asia, with lighter coverage in South America and Africa. This can help inform content localization strategies and regional growth initiatives. •Metadata Breadth: The dataset tracks 25 distinct age ratings, and the earliest recorded title dates back to 1920, which shows the platform’s commitment to both historical archiving and diverse viewer interests. Usefulness of the Dashboard •This dashboard serves as a strategic analytics tool for multiple teams: •Content Strategy & Curation: Helps in identifying which genres and age groups are over- or under-represented, guiding future acquisitions or production decisions. •Marketing & Personalization: Age rating and genre data can drive personalized content recommendations and age-appropriate promotional campaigns. •Regional Expansion: The country-level content breakdown supports decisions around regional investments and subtitle/dubbing prioritization. •Executive Decision-Making: Top-level summary stats (e.g., total titles, genre count, director diversity) provide quick KPI access for leadership reviews. •Trend Analysis: The release year visual can be used to monitor catalog freshness and historical content sourcing trends over time. Strategic Value: •By integrating multiple content dimensions into a single visual interface, this dashboard provides actionable intelligence for content operations, licensing, and business development. It enables Prime Video’s stakeholders to optimize content mix, strengthen market alignment, and enhance viewer satisfaction through data-driven decision-making.
