    Prime Video Content Analysis Dashboard — Comprehensive Write-Up

    This Power BI dashboard provides a multi-dimensional analysis of the Prime Video streaming catalog, highlighting critical insights into content distribution, genre diversity, audience targeting, and global reach. The intuitive visual layout supports both executive and operational decision-making.

    Key Highlights

    • Content Volume: The platform hosts a total of 9,655 titles, featuring 519 unique genres and 5,771 directors, underscoring the catalog's depth and diversity.
    • Format Split: A donut chart reveals that 80.82% of titles are movies, while 19.18% are TV shows. This indicates a strong focus on feature-length content, which is vital for long-form content strategy.
    • Temporal Distribution: The "Total Shows by Release Year" line chart illustrates a steep rise in content from the early 2000s, peaking around 2020–2021. This pattern helps track platform growth and content freshness.
    • Age Ratings: The "Ratings by Total Shows" bar chart shows that 13+, 16+, and All Ages are the most frequent classifications. This suggests a strong emphasis on content suitable for teens and families.
    • Genre Insights: Drama, Comedy, and hybrid categories like “Drama, Suspense” dominate. These findings are critical for content acquisition and original production planning.
    • Geographic Distribution: The “Total Shows by Country” map visual highlights strong content sourcing from North America, Europe, and parts of Asia, with lower representation from South America and Africa. These insights inform localization and international growth strategies.
    • Metadata Breadth: The dataset spans 25 distinct age ratings, with the oldest title dating back to 1920, reflecting Prime Video's inclusion of both historical and modern content.

    Usefulness of the Dashboard

    This dashboard provides tactical and strategic value for a wide range of teams:

    • Content Strategy & Curation: Identify over- and under-represented genres and age segments to guide future acquisition or production efforts.
    • Marketing & Personalization: Tailor campaigns and recommendation engines using insights from genre and age-rating distributions.
    • Regional Expansion: Use geographic breakdowns to support regional content investments and language localization (e.g., dubbing/subtitles).
    • Executive Decision-Making: Access high-level KPIs like total title count, genre variety, and director diversity at a glance.
    • Trend Analysis: Track catalog freshness and evolution through historical release-year data.

    Strategic Value

    By consolidating multiple content dimensions into a single dashboard, this tool enables data-driven decision-making across:

    • Content operations
    • Licensing
    • Viewer satisfaction
    • Business development

    It empowers Prime Video stakeholders to optimize content mix, align with market needs, and enhance user experience through actionable insights.
